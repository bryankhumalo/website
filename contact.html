Visiting Guide

Everything you need to know

A few details to help plan your visit to Christofa's.

🚗 Parking

Complimentary, secure guest parking is available behind the restaurant off Harbour Road, with an on-duty attendant during all lunch and dinner services.

🍷 Corkage Policy

Guests are welcome to bring special bottles of wine from their personal cellar. Corkage is R100 per 750ml bottle, limited to 2 bottles per table.

🌿 Dietary Options

Our kitchen caters attentively to vegetarian, pescatarian, halal-friendly, and gluten-free diets. Please note dietary preferences when booking.

👔 Dress Code

Coastal smart casual. While we are by the ocean, we kindly ask that guests refrain from beachwear and flip-flops for dinner services.

🎉 Private Events

Our sunlit courtyard terrace and private dining room are available for private celebrations and corporate gatherings for up to 45 guests.

💳 Payment Methods

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex) as well as Apple Pay, Google Pay, and contactless tap payments.
