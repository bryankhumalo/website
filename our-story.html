Our Story & Heritage

From one kitchen table to many

Before there was a menu, there was a grandmother's kitchen, a charcoal grill in the courtyard, and an unwavering rule that nobody leaves without eating first.

The roots of Christofa's

Christofa's began the way most true Greek cooking does — with more people at the door than chairs around the table. The recipes we cook today weren't invented in test kitchens; they were passed down across generations, shouted over sputtering charcoal, and adjusted to the rhythm of whoever sat down to eat.

When Christofa first tied his apron, the vision was simple: bring the warmth of a Mediterranean family dining room into a contemporary culinary space, where tradition isn't a museum piece but a living, breathing craft.

Every dish here is built for the middle of the table, not the corner of a plate — meant to be passed, argued over, and finished together.

The Philosophy

What we cook, and why

Our cooking rests on four pillars: wood fire, clean salt, cold-pressed olive oil, and wild mountain herbs.

  • The Line Fish: Caught fresh off the Cape coast every morning by small-boat fishermen. Never frozen, simply grilled over embers and dressed in lemon and oil.
  • The Wood Oven & Charcoal: Our wood oven burns throughout the day. Pork belly skewers, Karoo lamb, and sourdough pita get their distinctive char and sweetness from native wood embers.
  • Cold-Pressed Olive Oil: Sourced from small family groves. Oil good enough to drink, poured liberally over warm feta, ripe tomatoes, and bitter greens.
  • Wild Herbs: Mountain oregano, fresh dill, fragrant mint, and wild caper leaves that bring the salt wind of the Aegean into every bite.
“A short seasonal menu, cooked properly with patient hands, beats a long one cooked in a rush every single time.”
The Spirit of Philoxenia

A table where strangers become friends

The ancient Greek word philoxenia translates literally to "friend of the stranger" — a sacred commitment to making every person who walks through our doors feel as cherished as family.

At Christofa's, our dining room and sunlit courtyard terrace are designed around this very idea. Wine is poured generously, mezedes arrive hot as they are ready, and dinner unfolds at a relaxed, joyful pace.
