Our Heritage

From one kitchen table to many

Christofa's began the way all memorable Greek kitchens begin — with more family at the door than chairs at the table. Long before there were printed menus, there was a grandmother's kitchen, a wood fire in the courtyard, and an unspoken rule that nobody left without eating.

“Every dish here is built for the middle of the table, not the edge of a plate — meant to be passed, shared, argued over, and finished together.”

Today in Cape Town, we honour that same generous spirit. Our line fish is sourced daily from local coastal boats, our sourdough pita is baked each morning, and our olive oil comes from groves that know the sea wind.
